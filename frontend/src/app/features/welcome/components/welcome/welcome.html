Music Library

Your library, on your own terms.

A self-contained music library. It reads the tags off your own files, organises them into real artists and albums, and plays them back. No streaming service and no third-party account -- the only sign-in is this application's own.

Reads your tags

Embedded metadata is parsed on upload and reconciled against what is already there, so a second track from an album you own joins that album instead of creating a duplicate.

Real artists and albums

Artists and albums are first-class records, not strings copied onto every track. Renaming an artist is one change, and an album can be credited properly.

Search that spans the catalog

One box matches track titles, album titles, and artist names at once. Tracks with no album are still findable rather than quietly dropped.

Plays, and seeks

Audio is streamed with range-request support, so the scrubber works properly instead of only playing from the beginning.

No duplicates

A track is identified by a hash of its contents. Upload the same audio under a different filename and it is recognised, not copied.

Runs anywhere

One command brings up the database and the application together. No API keys to obtain and nothing to register for; the accounts it starts with are its own.
